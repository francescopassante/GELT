4. The training configuration, and what it cost to find
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- 120 epochs: no arm converged. Doubling to 240 moved every arm and
  FLIPPED THE SIGN OF M1 (+0.039 → −0.009), so the 120-epoch ranking was
  an artefact of the budget. The cosine has T_max = EPOCHS, so every
  horizon is a different experiment and rate optima do not transfer.
- The binding constraint was gradient STEPS, not epochs: 70 training
  configurations at batch = 4 is 18 steps per epoch. batch = 1 gives 70
  at the same wall clock and changed every reading by more than any rate
  did — frozen alone moved 0.17, from 0.5696 to 0.3992.
- Neither the batch size nor the L-CNN's conv_init_scale was recorded in
  any dump, stem or overwrite guard until this run. Two runs differing
  in them were indistinguishable in their artifacts. Both are recorded
  now (commits 'The batch size was an experiment axis no artifact
  recorded' and 'The L-CNN's init scale was in no dump either').
- conv_init_scale = 0.2 is a forward-only gate verdict, not an optimum;
  on V1 the L-CNN reads 0.6438 at 0.1 and 0.6320 at 0.2, within 2σ.
